# Lumen Grid Labs: Broader Impacts Plan (2025)

## Organizational Context and Objectives

Founded in 2019 in Golden, Colorado, Lumen Grid Labs, LLC is a 100% U.S.-owned clean tech enterprise employing 32 employees (UEI: LGLSYNTH0001). Under the leadership of CEO Dr. Maya Okafor, CTO Ravi Deshmukh, lead battery scientist Elena Brandt, and test engineer Jordan Lee, our team develops advanced power hardware and software systems. Our flagship innovations include the LGL-BMS3 solid-state battery management system (providing cell balancing to ±2 mV and state-of-charge estimation error ≤ 1.5%) and the HelioLink 250, a 250 kW silicon carbide grid-forming inverter delivering 98.7% peak efficiency.

Building upon our successful DOE SBIR Phase I 2023 award ("Degradation-aware BMS for second-life batteries", $200,000, all milestones met) and NSF SBIR Phase I 2024 award ("Grid-forming control for weak grids", $275,000, demonstrating 14% faster fault ride-through than baseline), this Broader Impacts Plan establishes structured workforce training and academic pathways across the Rocky Mountain region.

## University and Research Partnerships

Lumen Grid Labs maintains an active STTR research partnership with the Colorado School of Mines. Through this collaboration, graduate and undergraduate students participate in joint research using our 1,800 sq ft power-electronics lab. Students gain hands-on experience running dynamic emulation protocols on our 500 kW regenerative grid simulator and our OPAL-RT hardware-in-the-loop rig. Furthermore, our utility deployment roadmap is strengthened by our partner Front Range Utility Cooperative, who provided a formal letter of support in 2025 to evaluate grid-edge storage and power conversion applications in realistic operational environments.

## Technician Training with Regional Colleges

To address the acute regional shortage of certified power electronics and battery assembly technicians, Lumen Grid Labs is launching a technical apprenticeship program targeting Colorado community colleges. Dr. Maya Okafor will coordinate direct curriculum modules with vocational instructors, focusing on practical lab competence and real-world safety:

- **Laboratory Internships**: Sponsoring four paid student internships annually at our Golden facility, supervised by Jordan Lee and Ravi Deshmukh, focusing on hardware-in-the-loop validation and high-power inverter testing.
- **Applied Energy Curriculum**: Providing open-access instructional materials derived from HelioLink 250 inverter controls and LGL-BMS3 battery telemetry to Colorado community colleges to align vocational training with next-generation power infrastructure needs.
- **Continuing Education Seminars**: Hosting bi-monthly technical workshops led by Elena Brandt on battery pack safety, electrochemical balancing, and non-destructive testing.
