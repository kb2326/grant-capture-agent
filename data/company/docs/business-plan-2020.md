## Executive Summary
Founded in 2019 in Golden, Colorado, Lumen Grid Labs, LLC was established to bridge critical gaps between advanced battery chemistry and grid-scale power conversion. Modern electrical grids face unprecedented stability challenges as distributed renewables replace conventional rotational inertia. Our mission is to engineer high-performance battery management systems and silicon carbide power electronics that ensure grid reliability.

This business plan details our operational roadmap and capital requirements for our upcoming financing cycle as we transition from foundational laboratory architecture to commercial prototype validation.

## Technology and Product Vision
Our engineering roadmap targets two synergistic product lines:

1. **Advanced Battery Management Systems (BMS):** We are designing solid-state BMS architectures targeting cell balancing accuracy within ±2 mV and state-of-charge estimation error under 1.5%. By prioritizing thermal stability and precise cell tracking, our BMS technology addresses degradation in next-generation stationary storage.
2. **Silicon Carbide Grid-Forming Inverters:** We are laying the architectural foundation for compact, high-efficiency grid-forming inverters. Leveraging 1200 V silicon carbide (SiC) semiconductors, these systems will provide synthetic inertia, fast fault recovery, and autonomous voltage support for weak distribution feeders.

Through research partnerships with institutions such as the Colorado School of Mines, Lumen Grid Labs combines proprietary control algorithms with robust hardware design.
