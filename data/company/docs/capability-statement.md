# Lumen Grid Labs, LLC: Capability Statement

**Location:** Golden, Colorado | **Founded:** 2019 | **Employees:** 32 | **Ownership:** 100% U.S.-owned small business | **UEI:** LGLSYNTH0001

## Core capabilities

Lumen Grid Labs designs, builds and tests power electronics and battery controls for the electric grid. Our work falls into three areas.

1. **Battery management and estimation.** Cell balancing, state-of-charge and state-of-health estimation, and degradation-aware control, including packs built from retired electric-vehicle modules.
2. **Grid-forming power conversion.** Silicon-carbide inverters and the control software that lets them hold voltage and frequency on weak feeders and ride through faults.
3. **Hardware-in-the-loop validation.** Repeatable testing of controllers against simulated grids before any equipment is connected to a live feeder.

## Products

- **LGL-BMS3** is a solid-state battery management system. It balances cells to within plus or minus 2 mV and holds state-of-charge estimation error at or below 1.5%.
- **HelioLink 250** is a 250 kW silicon-carbide grid-forming inverter with 98.7% peak efficiency.

## Laboratory

Our 1,800 sq ft power-electronics lab in Golden includes a 500 kW regenerative grid simulator, an OPAL-RT hardware-in-the-loop rig, battery cyclers, thermal chambers, and calibrated measurement equipment. We also use the battery laboratory at Colorado School of Mines, our STTR research partner.

## Differentiators

- One team covers cell chemistry, battery estimation and inverter control, so the interfaces between them are designed together.
- Every controller is tested on the hardware-in-the-loop rig and the grid simulator before field use.
- We document test conditions in enough detail that results can be repeated by an independent party.

## Quality and compliance practices

We keep a written test plan for every high-power test and store raw measurement files with the test conditions, the instrument serial numbers and the firmware version that was running. This makes it possible to repeat a result months later or to hand the data to an independent reviewer. Firmware is kept under version control, and every release used in a test is tagged so that results can be traced to code. Lab safety follows NFPA 70E, and all staff who enter the high-power area complete arc flash training before their first day on the floor.
