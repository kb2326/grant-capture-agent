# Lumen Grid Labs Employee Handbook: Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

## Purpose and Overview
Lumen Grid Labs, LLC ("Lumen Grid Labs") recognizes the importance of personal well-being, rest, and work-life balance for our team in Golden, Colorado. As an organization of 32 employees, our goal is to maintain a predictable, fair, and flexible paid time off program that supports both individual employee needs and team continuity.

This policy outlines how paid time off is earned, tracked, and scheduled across all full-time regular personnel.

## PTO Accrual Rates
All full-time employees begin accruing paid leave on their first day of official employment. PTO accrual operates on a semi-monthly payroll schedule (24 pay periods per calendar year). Standard accrual rates are structured by years of continuous service:

- **Years 0 through 2:** 5.00 hours per pay period (equivalent to 15 days or 120 hours per calendar year).
- **Years 3 through 5:** 6.67 hours per pay period (equivalent to 20 days or 160 hours per calendar year).
- **Years 6 and above:** 8.33 hours per pay period (equivalent to 25 days or 200 hours per calendar year).

Part-time employees scheduled to work at least 20 hours per week receive prorated accruals based on their standard weekly hours. PTO does not accrue during unpaid leaves of absence lasting longer than 15 consecutive calendar days.

## Requesting Paid Time Off
To ensure laboratory workflows and test facilities maintain necessary staffing coverage, employees must plan and request time off in advance according to the following schedule:

1. **Planned Leaves (3 or more consecutive days):** Submit requests via the internal HR portal at least 10 business days prior to the planned start date.
2. **Short Leaves (1 to 2 days):** Submit requests at least 48 hours in advance.
3. **Unplanned Absences (Illness or Personal Emergencies):** Notify your immediate supervisor (such as Ravi Deshmukh, Elena Brandt, or your direct department manager) and Human Resources by 8:30 AM Mountain Time on the day of absence.

Supervisors review and approve PTO requests on a first-come, first-served basis, balancing personal preferences with laboratory coverage requirements.

## Carryover and Maximum Balances
Employees are strongly encouraged to utilize their earned time off each calendar year. The policy allows an employee to carry over a maximum of 40 hours of unused leave into the following calendar year, effective January 1. Any unused hours exceeding this 40-hour limit will be forfeited unless an exception is granted in writing by Dr. Maya Okafor prior to December 15.

Upon separation of employment in good standing, accrued but unused PTO will be paid out at the employee's standard base hourly rate on the final paycheck, subject to standard payroll withholdings.
