# Lumen Grid Labs Employee Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Purpose and Scope
This policy outlines the standard operating procedures for incurring, documenting, and submitting business expenses for reimbursement at Lumen Grid Labs, LLC. This policy applies to all 32 full-time and part-time employees based at our Golden, Colorado headquarters and remote work locations. The objective is to ensure that employees are promptly reimbursed for legitimate, necessary business expenses while maintaining clear financial records.

## Allowable Business Expenses
Employees may claim reimbursement for pre-approved business expenses incurred directly in the course of their daily duties. Common allowable categories include:
- **Travel & Lodging:** Standard hotel rooms up to $185 per night (excluding taxes), economy-class airfare booked at least 14 days in advance, and ground transportation (rideshare, taxi, or rental vehicles within standard midsize class limits).
- **Personal Vehicle Mileage:** Business travel using personal vehicles is reimbursed at the standard federal rate of $0.67 per mile. Commuting between home and the Golden office is not reimbursable.
- **Meals & Incidentals:** When traveling outside the Denver metro area on company business, meals are reimbursable up to a daily limit of $75 per day ($15 breakfast, $25 lunch, $35 dinner). Alcohol is not eligible for reimbursement.
- **Office & Field Supplies:** Incidental purchases of consumables, small hand tools, or office sundries under $100 necessary for immediate operations.

## Receipt and Documentation Standards
Itemized receipts are required for all expenditures exceeding $25.00. Credit card summary slips that lack item detail are not sufficient. Every submitted receipt must clearly display:
1. The name and location of the vendor.
2. The transaction date.
3. The itemized list of goods or services purchased.
4. The total amount paid and payment method.

If an original receipt is lost, the employee must make a reasonable effort to request a duplicate from the vendor. If unavailable, an explanatory memorandum approved by the department manager must accompany the claim.

## Submission and Approval Process
All claims must be submitted using the standard company template within 30 calendar days of incurring the cost.

1. **Compilation:** Consolidate receipts and itemize expenditures into a completed monthly expense report.
2. **Manager Review:** Submit the expense report to your immediate supervisor (e.g., Dr. Maya Okafor, Ravi Deshmukh, or your designated lab lead) for operational review and electronic signature.
3. **Finance Processing:** Forward the approved packet to the finance department at accounting@example.com.

Approved expense claims submitted by the 15th of the month will be reimbursed via direct deposit on the final payroll cycle of that month. Claims submitted after the 15th will be processed on the following payroll cycle.
