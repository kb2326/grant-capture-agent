# Lumen Grid Labs: Official Company Holiday Schedule 2025

## Policy Overview
Lumen Grid Labs, LLC observes eleven paid standard holidays throughout the 2025 calendar year. This policy applies to all 32 employees based at our headquarters and facility in Golden, Colorado. Paid holidays are provided to ensure our team has dedicated time for rest, family, and personal pursuits. Regular full-time employees receive full pay for designated company holidays, provided they work or have approved paid leave on their scheduled workday immediately preceding and following the holiday.

## Floating Holiday Guidelines
In addition to the fixed holiday schedule above, each full-time employee receives one paid floating holiday per calendar year. The floating holiday provides flexibility for employees to observe religious, cultural, or personal events that do not fall on standard company holidays.

Key guidelines for utilizing your floating holiday:
1. Floating holidays are credited on January 1 of each calendar year.
2. Newly hired employees who join Lumen Grid Labs prior to July 1 are eligible for the floating holiday during their initial year.
3. Requests to take a floating holiday should be submitted through the standard employee portal at least two weeks in advance, subject to supervisor approval.
4. Floating holidays do not roll over from one calendar year to the next and cannot be cashed out upon separation of employment.
