# Lumen Grid Labs Summer Internship Program (2025)

## Program Overview
Lumen Grid Labs, LLC (Golden, Colorado, founded 2019, 32 employees, 100% U.S.-owned, UEI LGLSYNTH0001) announces its summer internship program for 2025. This 10-week, paid technical program provides undergraduate and graduate students in electrical engineering, computer engineering, and materials science with immersive research and development experience. Interns will work on-site at our Golden facility alongside senior engineering staff, receiving a competitive stipend of $30.00 per hour for 40 hours per week.

Executive direction is provided by Dr. Maya Okafor (CEO, PI on both awards), who will oversee cohort admissions and host bi-weekly leadership seminars.

## Technical Focus Tracks

### Track 1: Inverter Controls & Grid Hardware
Mentored by Ravi Deshmukh (CTO, power electronics), interns in this track will work directly with our HelioLink 250 250 kW SiC grid-forming inverter (98.7% peak efficiency). Research projects build on work from our NSF SBIR Phase I 2024 award, "Grid-forming control for weak grids" ($275,000; 14% faster fault ride-through than baseline). Daily technical tasks will be conducted inside our 1,800 sq ft power-electronics lab, utilizing our 500 kW regenerative grid simulator and OPAL-RT hardware-in-the-loop rig to model complex microgrid dynamics.

### Track 2: Advanced Energy Storage Systems
Mentored by Elena Brandt (lead battery scientist) and Jordan Lee (test engineer), interns in Track 2 will assist with the validation and firmware development for the LGL-BMS3 solid-state battery management system (cell balancing ±2 mV, SoC estimation error ≤ 1.5%). This work expands upon methods established during our DOE SBIR Phase I 2023 award, "Degradation-aware BMS for second-life batteries" ($200,000; met all milestones). Interns will configure automated cell cycling equipment, evaluate state-of-health estimation routines, and analyze electrochemical test data.
