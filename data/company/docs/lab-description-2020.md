# Lumen Grid Labs: Laboratory Facility Description (2020)

**Document Date:** November 12, 2020  
**Facility Location:** Golden, Colorado  
**Author:** Ravi Deshmukh, CTO  

## Overview

This document outlines the testing footprint and laboratory assets for Lumen Grid Labs, LLC as of late 2020. Operating out of our initial commercial incubator lease in Golden, Colorado, the company maintains a dedicated 900 sq ft power-electronics and battery testing workshop. This space supports early-stage hardware prototyping, converter assembly, and low-voltage cell-characterization workflows led by Dr. Maya Okafor and our core technical team.

## Facility Layout and Physical Space

The facility comprises 900 sq ft of contiguous space partitioned into three primary operational bays:

1. **Converter Assembly & Prototyping Bay (400 sq ft):** Configured with static-dissipative workbenches, soldering stations, surface-mount rework tools, and precision mechanical assembly fixtures for custom printed circuit boards and silicon carbide gate drivers.
2. **Battery Testing & Cell Characterization Bay (300 sq ft):** Designed for individual cell balancing experiments, electrochemical evaluation, and pack-level staging. The area includes an insulated environmental bench capable of temperature regulation between -10°C and 60°C.
3. **Power Distribution & Electrical Enclosure (200 sq ft):** Houses centralized isolation transformers, safety disconnect switches, and early instrumentation racks.

## Electrical Service and Test Instrumentation

The 2020 facility is serviced by a 480V, 3-phase, 150 A electrical feed, providing up to 100 kVA of total testing capacity. Installed test equipment includes:

- **Programmable DC Power Supply:** Magna-Power 10 kW DC unit (0–500 V, 0–20 A) for simulating early battery string inputs.
- **Regenerative Electronic Load:** 15 kW bi-directional DC sink/source used for converter efficiency and step-response verification.
- **Battery Cycler:** 8-channel benchtop battery analyzer (0–5 V, 10 A per channel) dedicated to single-cell life-cycle and impedance testing.
- **Measurement & Data Acquisition:** Two 4-channel 500 MHz mixed-signal digital storage oscilloscopes, high-voltage differential probes (up to 1,500 V), and calibrated current transducers (up to 100 A RMS).

## Safety Protocols

Given the physical constraints of our current 900 sq ft laboratory footprint, strict test protocols govern all high-voltage operations. Battery cell cycling is limited to a maximum aggregate capacity of 2.5 kWh under active charge/discharge. The space is equipped with localized exhaust ventilation, class D dry-powder fire extinguishers, emergency power shutoff buttons at both exit doors, and clear line-of-sight safety interlocks across all test benches.
