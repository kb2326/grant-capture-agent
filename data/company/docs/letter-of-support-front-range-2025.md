## Scope and Support for the 2 MW Pilot Expansion

To bridge lab-scale hardware-in-the-loop validation and full utility distribution integration, Front Range Utility Cooperative commits to hosting the 2 MW pilot expansion at our regional distribution substation. Specifically, our cooperative will provide:
- Interconnection access and feeder integration for parallel HelioLink 250 units operating with grid-forming controls.
- Access to secondary utility battery banks managed by the LGL-BMS3 system.
- Operational telemetry and distribution feeder data collection during steady-state and transient grid events.
