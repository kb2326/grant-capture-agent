## 2. Technical Findings from 2021 Feeder Trials

Between January 2021 and August 2021, Boulder Valley Energy hosted Lumen Grid Labs' first-generation 45 kW Silicon Carbide inverter prototype and bench-scale pack monitor at our Valmont Substation testing compound. Testing focused on distribution voltage support, local power factor correction, and basic fault disconnection response.

Key observed performance metrics include:
- **Thermal Performance:** The liquid-cooled prototype maintained junction temperatures below 78°C under continuous 42 kW steady-state injection during ambient July heat conditions (36°C ambient).
- **Inverter Efficiency:** Benchmark power conversion efficiency reached an average of 96.4% across variable load bands ranging from 10 kW to 45 kW.
- **Sensor Accuracy:** The preliminary pack sensing module demonstrated state-of-charge tracking variance within 3.8% across 120 charge-discharge cycles on lithium iron phosphate cells.
- **Response Speed:** Active power curtailment response times averaged 42 milliseconds upon receiving simulated feeder droop signals.

At the time of this evaluation, Lumen Grid Labs was operating with a core team of 8 employees. Dr. Maya Okafor and lead engineer Ravi Deshmukh provided exemplary technical leadership, maintaining safe operating conditions throughout all 320 hours of energized field testing.
