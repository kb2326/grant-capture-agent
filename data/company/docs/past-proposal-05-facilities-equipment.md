# Facilities, Equipment and Resources

Lumen Grid Labs, LLC operates from a single site in Golden, Colorado. This section was used in both the 2023 DOE and 2024 NSF proposals.

## Power-electronics laboratory

The laboratory covers 1,800 sq ft and is wired for three-phase power at 480 V. It has separate benches for low-voltage control work, for battery packs and for high-power inverter testing, with a marked safety perimeter around the high-power area.

## Major equipment

- 500 kW regenerative grid simulator, able to emulate weak grids, sags, phase jumps and faults.
- OPAL-RT hardware-in-the-loop rig for real-time simulation of feeders and controllers.
- Battery cyclers for packs up to 100 V, and thermal chambers for cycling at set temperatures.
- Power analyzers, high-bandwidth current probes and differential voltage probes, calibrated annually.
- Prototype assembly area for the LGL-BMS3 and the HelioLink 250, including a reflow oven and inspection microscope.

## Safety practices

Every new employee completes high-voltage safety and arc flash training (NFPA 70E) before entering the lab. Jordan Lee is the lab safety lead. High-power tests require a written test plan, two people present, a lockout and tagout check, and a review of the plan by Ravi Deshmukh. Battery packs are cycled in a ventilated area with fire suppression and temperature cutoffs. Incidents and near misses are logged and reviewed monthly.

## Access to the Colorado School of Mines laboratory

As the STTR research partner, Lumen Grid Labs has access to the battery characterization laboratory at Colorado School of Mines. It includes impedance spectroscopy equipment and long-duration cell testers that supplement our own cyclers. Access is arranged through a standing agreement, and Dr. Elena Brandt coordinates scheduling.

## Other resources

The company has a software team that maintains firmware and data analysis tools, a shipping and receiving area, and enough space to hold retired battery modules before testing. No additional facilities are needed for the proposed work.
