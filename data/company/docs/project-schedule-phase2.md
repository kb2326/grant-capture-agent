# Phase II Project Schedule: Advanced Energy Storage & Grid-Forming Integration (2025)

## Project Overview & Scope

Lumen Grid Labs, LLC (Golden, Colorado; founded 2019; 32 employees; 100% U.S.-owned; UEI LGLSYNTH0001) establishes this work breakdown structure and execution plan for Phase II commercialization. Following successful performance on DOE SBIR Phase I 2023, "Degradation-aware BMS for second-life batteries" ($200,000; met all milestones) and NSF SBIR Phase I 2024, "Grid-forming control for weak grids" ($275,000; 14% faster fault ride-through than baseline), this project integrates advanced storage management with high-efficiency grid-forming inverters.

Primary development leverages the company's 1,800 sq ft power-electronics lab, utilizing our 500 kW regenerative grid simulator and OPAL-RT hardware-in-the-loop rig. Testing will center on the **LGL-BMS3** solid-state battery management system (cell balancing ±2 mV, SoC estimation error ≤ 1.5%) coupled to the **HelioLink 250** 250 kW SiC grid-forming inverter (98.7% peak efficiency). Work will be conducted in partnership with research collaborator Colorado School of Mines and utility partner Front Range Utility Cooperative (letter of support 2025).
