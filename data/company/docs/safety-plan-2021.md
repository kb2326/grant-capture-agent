## 2. Electrical Safety and NFPA 70E-2018 Compliance
Personnel must treat all electrical assemblies as energized until a zero-energy state is formally verified using calibrated multimeters. Work conducted on energized circuits must strictly adhere to NFPA 70E-2018 standards for electrical safety in the workplace.

Mandatory compliance requirements include:
- **Boundaries:** Arc flash and shock approach boundaries designated under NFPA 70E-2018 must be clearly demarked with yellow-and-black floor striping around the 60 kW DC bench power supply.
- **Personal Protective Equipment (PPE):** Arc-rated face shields (minimum 8 cal/cm² rating), NFPA-compliant safety glasses with side shields, and Class 0 (1,000 V rated) insulating rubber gloves with leather outer protectors are mandatory within the restricted approach boundary.
- **Lockout/Tagout (LOTO):** Energy isolation devices on the primary 208 V 3-phase wall disconnect and prototype DC bus links must be locked and tagged out before any manual probe adjustment or lead relocation.
- **Two-Person Rule:** Any active test exceeding 100 V DC or 20 A requires a second qualified team member trained in CPR and first aid to be present within direct line of sight.

## 3. Battery Bench and Hardware Protocols
When handling prototype cell blocks and early battery management hardware:
- Static-dissipative bench mats (surface resistivity 10^6 to 10^9 ohms/sq) must be grounded to the building earth.
- Technicians must use fully insulated torque wrenches and hand tools to prevent accidental bridging across terminal lugs.
- A dedicated Class D extinguishing agent and a standard 10 lb ABC dry-chemical extinguisher must be located within 15 feet of the battery assembly table.
- Thermal imaging inspections must occur at 15-minute intervals during initial charge and discharge cycles.
