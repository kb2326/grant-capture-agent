# Lumen Grid Labs - SparkCube Home Charger Flyer (Discontinued Product Archive)

## Product Overview: SparkCube Dual-Port Wall Charger

**Notice: This consumer accessory was discontinued on October 14, 2022. This document is retained for internal record-keeping and archive purposes only.**

Between January 2021 and October 2022, Lumen Grid Labs, LLC briefly resold the **SparkCube** as a branded consumer accessory. The SparkCube was an ultra-compact 65 W dual-port Gallium Nitride (GaN) fast wall charger designed for phones, tablets, and lightweight ultrabooks. Distributed primarily through regional promotional bundles and internal merchandise packages at our Golden, Colorado headquarters, the SparkCube offered reliable, compact desktop power delivery before our operational focus shifted entirely to utility-scale power electronics and advanced battery management.

### Key Product Features
- **Dual USB-C Power Delivery**: Capable of outputting up to 65 W via a single port or dynamically splitting power (45 W and 20 W) when charging two mobile devices simultaneously.
- **GaN Power Conversion**: High-frequency Gallium Nitride switching architecture allowed a chassis footprint 48% smaller than typical silicone-based OEM laptop bricks.
- **Folding Prongs**: Retractable North American wall blades designed for compact travel.
- **Universal Protocol Compatibility**: Supported USB-PD 3.0, PPS (Programmable Power Supply), and Quick Charge 4+ profiles.

## Discontinuation & Inventory Disposition

In late 2022, executive leadership—including CEO Dr. Maya Okafor and CTO Ravi Deshmukh—realigned company resources to concentrate exclusively on core R&D platforms, namely the LGL-BMS3 battery management system and the HelioLink 250 grid-forming inverter. As Lumen Grid Labs expanded to 32 employees and focused its 1,800 sq ft power-electronics lab on utility-scale systems, peripheral consumer hardware reselling was permanently ended.

- **End of Resale**: October 14, 2022
- **Final Inventory Depletion**: The remaining 142 warehouse units were distributed to employees or responsibly decommissioned via certified local electronics recyclers.
- **End of Warranty Support**: Customer service warranty coverage and replacement claim handling for the SparkCube formally concluded on December 31, 2023.
