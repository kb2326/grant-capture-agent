# Lumen Grid Labs Business Travel and Expense Policy

## 1. Purpose and Scope
This document outlines the standard travel and expense reimbursement procedures for all 32 employees of Lumen Grid Labs, LLC, headquartered in Golden, Colorado. The policy ensures fair, consistent, and accountable management of business-related travel expenses incurred during company operations, customer visits, vendor meetings, and technical conferences.

## 2. Pre-Travel Authorization
All employees must obtain written or electronic authorization before booking non-local travel:
- **Domestic Travel (under $1,500):** Requires written approval from the appropriate functional lead (such as CTO Ravi Deshmukh or lead battery scientist Elena Brandt) at least seven business days prior to departure.
- **Domestic Travel ($1,500 and above) and All International Travel:** Requires formal approval from CEO Dr. Maya Okafor at least 14 business days prior to departure.

Test engineers and project staff traveling for field testing or partner visits must submit an estimated travel budget itemizing airfare, lodging, and ground transportation.

## 3. Transportation Guidelines
- **Commercial Air Travel:** All flights must be booked in economy or standard coach class. Employees should make reservations at least 14 calendar days in advance whenever practical to obtain cost-effective fares. Seat upgrades, priority boarding, and flexible refundable fares require prior written approval.
- **Ground Transportation:** The use of rideshare services, public transit, or airport shuttles is encouraged. Rental cars are permitted when public transit or rideshare is impractical; standard midsize or compact vehicles should be selected.
- **Personal Vehicle Usage:** When an employee utilizes a personal vehicle for authorized business travel outside the Denver–Golden metropolitan area, reimbursement is provided at the standard IRS mileage rate ($0.67 per mile). Commuting between home and the Golden facility is not reimbursable.

## 4. Lodging and Lodging Limits
Employees must reserve standard single-occupancy hotel rooms. Accommodations should be booked at the conference venue rate or within standard General Services Administration (GSA) rate guidelines for the destination city. Itemized hotel folios showing a zero balance must be retained and submitted. Personal expenses such as room service, minibar items, and pay-per-view media are non-reimbursable.

## 5. Meals and Incidental Expenses
Lumen Grid Labs uses standard federal GSA rates to establish meal allowances. Employees may claim meal expenses using one of the following methods:
- **Per Diem Allowance:** Travelers may request the standard per diem rate applicable to the travel destination for each full day of travel. Partial travel days (departure and return days) are reimbursed at 75% of the standard per diem rate. Detailed receipts are not required when claiming the standard per diem allowance, provided travel dates are verified.
- **Actual Expenses:** If actual expense reimbursement is elected, employees must provide itemized receipts for each meal. Total daily meal costs cannot exceed the destination per diem rate. Alcohol purchases are strictly non-reimbursable.

## 6. Expense Reporting and Reimbursement
All expense reports must be submitted via the internal accounting portal within 14 calendar days following the conclusion of travel. Reports must include itemized electronic receipts for any expense of $25.00 or greater. Approved reimbursements will be issued via direct deposit within two standard payroll cycles.
