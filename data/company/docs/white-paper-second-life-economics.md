## Executive Summary

As the deployment of electric vehicles accelerates, retired automotive traction packs present a compelling, sustainable alternative for stationary grid storage. Lumen Grid Labs, LLC—a 100% U.S.-owned clean energy research firm founded in 2019 with 32 employees—has developed advanced power electronics and controls to capture this value. This paper presents an economic evaluation of second-life battery packs managed by our proprietary **LGL-BMS3** solid-state battery management system versus newly manufactured stationary battery packs. Our findings demonstrate that integrating decommissioned packs using the LGL-BMS3 achieves a **38% lower cost per kWh** compared to new utility-grade installations, while preserving operational reliability and safety.

## Cost Structure Comparison: Second-Life vs. New Battery Assets

Stationary energy storage deployments using virgin lithium-ion cells face elevated capital expenses, with fully packaged new racks averaging $138/kWh in 2024–2025 capital equipment markets. In contrast, retired EV packs can be acquired at module scale for approximately $42/kWh. 

Historically, the barrier to repurposing automotive packs has been non-uniform cell degradation and high integration overhead. Lumen Grid Labs resolves this through automated grading and the LGL-BMS3 architecture:

- **Pack Sourcing & Ingestion:** $42.00/kWh
- **Grading, Disassembly & Recertification:** $16.50/kWh
- **LGL-BMS3 Electronics & Integration:** $27.00/kWh
- **Total Packaged Capital Cost:** $85.50/kWh

Compared to $138.00/kWh for baseline new systems, the deployed second-life pack achieves a **38% lower cost per kWh**. 

## Technical Enablement via LGL-BMS3

This cost reduction is only viable when cycle life is maximized despite cell-to-cell capacity variance. Built upon outcomes from our DOE SBIR Phase I 2023 project ("Degradation-aware BMS for second-life batteries", $200,000; met all milestones), the LGL-BMS3 delivers:

- Active cell balancing to within **±2 mV**, preventing premature string cutoff.
- State-of-Charge (SoC) estimation error **≤ 1.5%**, optimizing usable capacity windows.

Hardware validation was completed in our 1,800 sq ft power-electronics lab utilizing our 500 kW regenerative grid simulator and OPAL-RT hardware-in-the-loop rig.
