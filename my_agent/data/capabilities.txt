## 1. CORE COMPETENCIES (PRIMARY SEARCH DOMAINS)

### Waste-to-Energy & Advanced Thermal Conversion
- **Flagship Tech**: TURNW2E® Rotary Kiln Gasification System.
- **Capacity**: 5–100 tons/day of mixed solid waste.
- **Feedstocks**: Plastics, biomass, municipal solid waste (MSW), agricultural residues.
- **Outputs**: Syngas, heat, renewable fuels.
- **Target Environments**: DoD installations, disaster zones, remote communities.

### Bioenergy & Renewable Natural Gas (RNG)
- **Systems**: Anaerobic & Aerobic Digestion.
- **Inputs**: Food waste, biomass, organic residues.
- **Outputs**: Biogas, biomethane, upgraded RNG (pipeline or on-site).
- **Features**: Modular, containerized systems.

### Carbonless Fuels (Hydrogen & Ammonia)
- **Focus**: Distributed systems for renewable hydrogen and carbon-free ammonia.
- **Integration**: Microgrids, renewable sources, storage.
- **Methods**: CO2 conversion pathways to low-carbon fuels.

### Distributed Microgrids & Energy Systems
- **Components**: EV charging, battery storage, solar, W2E, hydrogen systems.
- **Management**: Real-time energy management.
- **Use Case**: Energy resilience, emergency ops, Forward Operating Bases (FOBs).

### Atmospheric Water Generation (AWG)
- **Tech**: Low-energy water harvesting from air.
- **Output**: Potable drinking water.
- **Power**: Solar or microgrid-integrated.

### Carbon Capture & Emissions Reduction
- **Goals**: CO2 sequestration, Methane capture (preventing gas-flares).
- **Mandates**: Supports U.S. federal climate/resilience mandates.

### Biomass-Based & Sustainable Materials
- **Products**: 100% biomass-based, PLA-free, PFAS-free food packaging.
- **Applications**: Military food service, national parks, disaster response.

## 3. DETAILED PROJECT VERTICALS (FOR DEEP MATCHING)

### Vertical A: PFAS Remediation & Soil Health
- **Technologies**: Biochar-derived graphene, Graphene oxide (GO) sorbents, Nanostructured adsorbents.
- **Applications**: 
  - Wastewater/Drinking water treatment.
  - Biosolids separation & management.
  - Soil remediation (immobilization/capture).
- **Keywords**: PFAS destruction, agricultural biosolids, soil carbon stabilization.

### Vertical B: Water Harvesting & Recovery
- **Technologies**: 
  - Wet Air Oxidation (WAO).
  - Biodigester wastewater processing.
  - Atmospheric water harvesting (IDM).
- **Processes**: Sludge-to-water conversion, Oxidative water recovery.

### Vertical C: Methane to Methanol Conversion
- **Technologies**: 
  - Direct electrocatalytic methane-to-methanol (DECMMT).
  - Dielectric barrier discharge (DBD) plasma reactor.
  - Non-thermal plasma conversion.
- **Feed Sources**: Landfill gas, Biodigesters, Oil/Gas flares.

### Vertical D: Agricultural Innovations (USDA Focus)
- **Edible Biocoatings**: Chitosan/Lignin nanoparticles for shelf-life extension.
- **Sugarcane/Bagasse Energy**: Combustion/CHP for distilleries and farms.
